[TOPIC: Purpose and Scope]

This demonstration document describes general concepts associated with rehabilitation and resettlement of persons affected by land acquisition and mining-related development.

This is synthetic development content and is not an official legal text.


[TOPIC: Affected Families]

Affected families may include persons whose land, residence, livelihood, or other recognized interests are affected by a project.

The identification of affected families is an important part of rehabilitation and resettlement planning.


[TOPIC: Displacement]

Project activities may result in displacement of people from their homes or places of livelihood.

Where displacement occurs, appropriate rehabilitation and resettlement measures should be considered according to applicable requirements.


[TOPIC: Rehabilitation]

Rehabilitation measures are intended to help affected persons recover or improve their living conditions and livelihoods after project-related impacts.

Measures may depend on the nature and extent of the impact.


[TOPIC: Resettlement]

Resettlement may involve providing suitable arrangements for affected persons who need to relocate.

Planning should consider housing, basic services, access, and other relevant needs.


[TOPIC: Livelihood Support]

Projects may affect the livelihoods of persons dependent on acquired land or local economic activities.

Rehabilitation measures may include assistance intended to support restoration or development of livelihoods.


[TOPIC: Housing]

Where affected persons are required to relocate, appropriate housing arrangements may be considered.

Resettlement planning should take into account access to basic services and suitable living conditions.


[TOPIC: Community Facilities]

Resettlement areas may require access to community facilities and basic services.

These may include roads, water supply, sanitation, education, healthcare, and other necessary facilities.


[TOPIC: Consultation]

Affected persons should be provided appropriate opportunities to receive information and participate in consultation processes where required.

Consultation can help identify concerns and improve rehabilitation and resettlement planning.


[TOPIC: Grievance Handling]

A grievance mechanism may be established so that affected persons can raise concerns regarding rehabilitation and resettlement.

Complaints should be recorded and addressed through an appropriate process.


[TOPIC: Monitoring]

Rehabilitation and resettlement measures should be monitored to determine whether planned support is being provided.

Monitoring may include progress of housing, livelihood support, infrastructure, and resolution of grievances.


[TOPIC: Documentation]

Records should be maintained regarding affected families, rehabilitation measures, resettlement arrangements, compensation, grievances, and monitoring activities.

Proper documentation supports accountability and evaluation.
