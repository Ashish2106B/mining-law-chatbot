TITLE
Rehabilitation and Resettlement - Demonstration Document

PURPOSE
This document describes general concepts associated with rehabilitation and resettlement of persons affected by land acquisition or development projects.

SCOPE
The document covers:
- affected families
- displacement
- rehabilitation
- resettlement
- livelihood support
- housing
- community facilities
- consultation

AFFECTED FAMILIES
An affected family may include persons whose land, residence, livelihood or other interests are affected by a development or acquisition process.

DISPLACEMENT
Displacement may occur when affected persons are required to move because of a project.

REHABILITATION
Rehabilitation aims to help affected persons restore or improve their living conditions and livelihoods.

RESETTLEMENT
Resettlement involves arrangements for relocating affected persons where relocation is required.

LIVELIHOOD
Where applicable, rehabilitation measures may address the loss of livelihood caused by acquisition or displacement.

HOUSING
Where the applicable framework provides housing assistance or other housing-related benefits, these should be implemented according to the prescribed procedure.

COMMUNITY FACILITIES
Resettlement planning may consider access to:
- roads
- drinking water
- sanitation
- education
- healthcare
- electricity
- community facilities

CONSULTATION
Affected persons may be consulted during the planning and implementation of rehabilitation and resettlement measures where required.

GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Affected persons should have access to the grievance mechanism provided under the applicable legal framework.

DOCUMENTATION
Records should be maintained concerning affected persons, rehabilitation measures, payments and resettlement arrangements.

MONITORING
Implementation of rehabilitation and resettlement measures should be monitored to identify delays or unresolved issues.
