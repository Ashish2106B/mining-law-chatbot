Land Acquisition - Demonstration Document

PURPOSE
This demonstration document describes general concepts associated with acquisition of private land for public or development purposes.

SCOPE
The document covers:
- acquisition process
- notices
- affected persons
- land records
- compensation
- objections
- possession
- rehabilitation

LAND IDENTIFICATION
The acquisition process begins with identification of land required for the proposed purpose.

LAND RECORDS
Relevant land records should be examined to identify owners and persons having interests in the land.

NOTICE
Affected persons may receive notices according to the applicable legal procedure.

OBJECTIONS
Where the applicable law provides an opportunity for objections, affected persons may submit objections within the prescribed process.

ASSESSMENT
The acquiring authority may assess the land and interests affected by the proposed acquisition.

COMPENSATION
Compensation may be determined according to the applicable legal framework.

Factors relevant to compensation depend on the applicable legislation and circumstances.

AWARD
The competent authority may determine the compensation payable through the prescribed procedure.

POSSESSION
Possession of acquired land should be taken according to the applicable legal requirements.

AFFECTED FAMILIES
Acquisition may affect landowners, tenants, workers and other persons depending on their relationship with the land.

REHABILITATION AND RESETTLEMENT
Where applicable, affected persons may be entitled to rehabilitation or resettlement measures.

DOCUMENTATION
Authorities and affected persons should maintain relevant documents relating to ownership, notices, compensation and other acquisition proceedings.
