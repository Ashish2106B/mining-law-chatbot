[TOPIC: Safety Awareness]

This demonstration circular provides general safety guidance for mining operations.

Mining organizations should promote safety awareness among workers, supervisors, and management.

This is synthetic development content and does not represent an official DGMS circular.


[TOPIC: Accident Prevention]

Mining accidents can be reduced by identifying hazards and implementing appropriate preventive measures.

Management should review unsafe conditions and take corrective action before they result in accidents.


[TOPIC: Machinery Safety]

Mining machinery should be operated only by trained and authorized personnel.

Machines should be inspected and maintained regularly.

Workers should remain clear of dangerous moving parts and follow established operating procedures.


[TOPIC: Personal Protective Equipment]

Workers should use appropriate personal protective equipment when required.

Depending on the work environment, protective equipment may include helmets, safety footwear, eye protection, hearing protection, respiratory protection, and other suitable equipment.


[TOPIC: Emergency Preparedness]

Mine management should maintain emergency preparedness arrangements.

Workers should know evacuation procedures, emergency communication methods, assembly locations, and responsibilities during emergencies.


[TOPIC: Accident Reporting]

Accidents and dangerous incidents should be reported through appropriate procedures.

Reports should contain sufficient information to support investigation and identification of preventive measures.


[TOPIC: Lessons from Accidents]

Mining accidents should be investigated to identify their causes and contributing factors.

Lessons learned from accidents can be communicated to workers to help prevent similar incidents.


[TOPIC: Safety Training]

Workers should receive regular safety training.

Training should be relevant to the worker's duties and may include hazard identification, equipment safety, emergency response, and safe working practices.
