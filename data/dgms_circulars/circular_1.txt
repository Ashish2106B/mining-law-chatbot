TITLE
DGMS Safety Circular - Demonstration Document

SUBJECT
Safety awareness and prevention of accidents in mining operations.

PURPOSE
This demonstration circular provides example safety guidance for mine operators and workers.

SAFETY AWARENESS
Mine management should regularly communicate important safety information to workers.

Safety meetings may be conducted to discuss:
- recent incidents
- identified hazards
- corrective actions
- emergency procedures
- safe working practices

ACCIDENT PREVENTION
Mine operators should identify hazards before work begins.

Workers should report unsafe conditions to supervisors or the responsible authority.

MACHINERY SAFETY
Workers should not operate machinery unless they are appropriately trained and authorized.

Machines should be inspected and maintained according to an appropriate maintenance schedule.

PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT
Workers should use protective equipment appropriate to the hazards of their work.

Examples include:
- helmets
- safety footwear
- eye protection
- hearing protection
- respiratory protection

EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS
Mines should maintain suitable emergency procedures.

Emergency procedures should be communicated to workers and periodically reviewed.

REPORTING
Accidents, dangerous occurrences and unsafe conditions should be appropriately documented.

LESSONS FROM ACCIDENTS
Mine management should examine incidents to identify their causes and prevent similar incidents from occurring again.

TRAINING
Safety training should be provided to workers according to their duties and identified hazards.
