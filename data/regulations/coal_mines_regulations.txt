[TOPIC: Ventilation]

Adequate ventilation is important for maintaining safe conditions in underground mining operations.

Ventilation systems should provide sufficient airflow and help control harmful gases, heat, smoke, and airborne contaminants.


[TOPIC: Gas Monitoring]

Mining operations should monitor gases that may create unsafe conditions.

Gas monitoring equipment should be maintained properly and workers should be trained to respond when dangerous gas levels are detected.


[TOPIC: Dust Control]

Mining operations should take measures to control dust generated during mining activities.

Dust control measures may include water application, ventilation, equipment controls, and other appropriate methods.


[TOPIC: Ground Control]

Ground and roof conditions should be monitored to reduce the risk of falls of ground.

Appropriate support systems and inspection procedures should be used where required by mining conditions.


[TOPIC: Machinery Safety]

Mining machinery should be operated and maintained using appropriate safety procedures.

Workers operating machinery should receive appropriate training and should follow established operating and maintenance procedures.


[TOPIC: Electrical Safety]

Electrical equipment used in mines should be installed, operated, and maintained using appropriate safety procedures.

Electrical hazards should be identified and workers should be protected against electric shock, fire, and other electrical risks.


[TOPIC: Worker Protection]

Workers should be protected from hazards associated with mining operations.

Appropriate protective equipment, safety procedures, training, supervision, and hazard controls should be provided.


[TOPIC: Emergency Procedures]

Mines should maintain procedures for responding to emergencies.

Emergency procedures may cover evacuation, communication, rescue operations, emergency equipment, and responsibilities of mine personnel.


[TOPIC: Mine Inspections]

Mining areas and safety systems should be inspected regularly.

Inspections may cover ventilation, roof conditions, machinery, electrical equipment, working areas, and other safety-related conditions.


[TOPIC: Safety Training]

Workers should receive training appropriate to their duties.
