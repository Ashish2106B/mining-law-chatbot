PURPOSE

VENTILATION
Adequate ventilation is important in underground mining.

Ventilation systems may be used to:
- provide fresh air
- control harmful gases
- control dust
- regulate environmental conditions

GASES
Mining operations may encounter hazardous gases.

Gas monitoring and appropriate safety procedures should be used where gas hazards may exist.

DUST CONTROL
Coal dust can create occupational and operational hazards.

Measures for controlling dust may include:
- water spraying
- ventilation
- dust monitoring
- suitable respiratory protection

ROOF AND GROUND CONTROL
Underground mine workings should be maintained to reduce the risk of ground or roof failure.

Suitable support systems should be used according to the conditions of the mine.

MACHINERY
Mining machinery should be operated and maintained according to appropriate safety procedures.

Workers operating machinery should receive suitable training.

ELECTRICAL SAFETY
Electrical equipment used in mining should be appropriately maintained.

Electrical hazards may include:
- electric shock
- short circuits
- damaged cables
- faulty equipment

WORKER PROTECTION
Workers should use appropriate protective equipment for the hazards associated with their work.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
Emergency plans should cover foreseeable mining emergencies.

Emergency arrangements may include:
- communication systems
- evacuation procedures
- first aid
- emergency equipment
- rescue arrangements

INSPECTIONS
Mine workings, equipment and safety systems should be inspected periodically.

Defects identified during inspections should be addressed through an appropriate corrective process.
