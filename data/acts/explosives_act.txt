Explosives Act - Demonstration Document

PURPOSE
This demonstration document describes general concepts relating to the safe handling and management of explosives used in mining activities.

EXPLOSIVES IN MINING
Explosives may be used in mining operations for controlled blasting and excavation.

Because explosives present significant hazards, their use should be controlled through appropriate procedures.

AUTHORIZED HANDLING
Only appropriately authorized and trained personnel should handle explosives.

Workers should understand the hazards associated with explosives before participating in blasting activities.

STORAGE
Explosives should be stored in designated and appropriately secured storage facilities.

Storage arrangements should consider:
- unauthorized access
- fire risks
- separation of incompatible materials
- inventory control
- security

TRANSPORTATION
Explosives should be transported using suitable procedures and equipment.

Transportation procedures should minimize the possibility of:
- accidental ignition
- unauthorized access
- damage to explosive materials
- loss of materials

HANDLING
Explosives should be handled carefully to prevent accidental ignition or damage.

Personnel should follow established procedures for loading, unloading and transferring explosives.

BLASTING OPERATIONS
Blasting should be carried out according to an approved procedure.

Before blasting, personnel should identify the danger zone and take measures to keep unauthorized persons away.

WARNING PROCEDURES
Appropriate warning procedures should be followed before blasting.

Workers and other persons in affected areas should be informed when necessary.

MISFIRES
A misfire is a blasting event in which an explosive charge does not function as intended.

Misfires should be treated as hazardous conditions.

Personnel should follow an established misfire procedure and should not approach the affected area without appropriate authorization.

RECORD KEEPING
Records relating to the receipt, storage, issue and use of explosives should be maintained where required.

EMERGENCY RESPONSE
Emergency procedures should be available for incidents involving explosives.

Possible emergencies include:
- accidental ignition
- fire
- explosion
- suspected misfire
- loss of explosives
- unauthorized access
