COAL MINE OPERATIONS
Coal mining operations may include exploration, excavation, transportation, processing and storage of coal.

Mining activities should be planned and conducted in a manner that considers worker safety and operational risks.

MINE MANAGEMENT
The mine management is responsible for establishing appropriate procedures for safe mining operations.

Management should ensure that workers receive suitable instructions before performing hazardous activities.

SAFETY MANAGEMENT
A mine should have procedures for identifying and controlling workplace hazards.

Safety management may include:
- hazard identification
- risk assessment
- safety inspections
- worker training
- emergency planning
- equipment maintenance
- supervision of hazardous activities

WORKER SAFETY
Workers should be informed about hazards associated with their work.

Workers should follow established safety procedures and use the protective equipment required for their activities.

SUPERVISION
Mining activities involving significant hazards should be appropriately supervised.

Supervisors should ensure that workers understand applicable operating and safety procedures.

MINE INSPECTIONS
Mining areas and equipment should be periodically inspected.

Inspections may identify:
- unsafe working conditions
- damaged equipment
- inadequate ventilation
- electrical hazards
- structural hazards
- other operational risks

EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
Mine operators should maintain procedures for responding to emergencies.

Possible emergencies include:
- fire
- explosion
- flooding
- roof or structural failure
- equipment accidents
- ventilation failure

ACCIDENT REPORTING
Significant accidents and dangerous occurrences should be recorded.

Where applicable, relevant incidents should be reported to the appropriate authority according to the applicable legal requirements.

WORKER WELFARE
Mining operations should provide suitable welfare facilities.

- drinking water
- sanitation
- rest facilities
- first-aid arrangements
- suitable working conditions
