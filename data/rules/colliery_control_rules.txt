This demonstration document describes general requirements associated with records, production, dispatch, weighing, stock management, and reporting in colliery operations.

[TOPIC: Colliery Records]

Collieries should maintain records relating to mining and coal production activities.

Records may include production quantities, dispatch information, stock information, weighing information, and other operational details.


[TOPIC: Production Records]

Production records should accurately reflect the quantity of coal produced by mining operations.

Records should be maintained regularly and should be available for inspection when required.


[TOPIC: Coal Dispatch]

Information about coal dispatched from a colliery should be recorded.

Dispatch records may include the quantity of coal dispatched, date of dispatch, destination, and other relevant details.


[TOPIC: Weighing]

Coal quantities should be determined using appropriate weighing arrangements.

Weighing records should be maintained so that coal production, stock, and dispatch quantities can be compared and verified.


[TOPIC: Stock Management]

Collieries should maintain information about coal stock.

Stock records help compare coal produced, coal dispatched, and coal remaining at the mining operation.


[TOPIC: Reporting]

Operational information should be reported to the appropriate responsible authority or management according to applicable procedures.

Reports should be accurate, complete, and maintained in an organized manner.


[TOPIC: Supervision]

Responsible personnel should supervise the maintenance of operational records.

Supervision helps ensure that production, dispatch, weighing, and stock information is recorded correctly.


[TOPIC: Record Maintenance]

Records should be maintained for an appropriate period according to applicable requirements.

Records should be organized so that relevant information can be retrieved when required for inspection, verification, or reporting.
