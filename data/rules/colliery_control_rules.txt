TITLE
Colliery Control Rules - Demonstration Document

PURPOSE
This document describes example controls associated with the operation and management of a colliery.

SCOPE
The demonstration rules cover:
- mine records
- production information
- coal dispatch
- weighing
- reporting
- operational supervision

COLLIERY
A colliery is a mining operation associated with the extraction and handling of coal.

MINE RECORDS
Mine operators should maintain accurate records relating to mining activities.

Records may include:
- production
- dispatch
- stock
- workforce
- equipment
- inspections

PRODUCTION RECORDS
Information about coal production should be recorded accurately.

Production records may be used to monitor mine operations and prepare required reports.

COAL DISPATCH
Coal dispatched from a mine should be properly recorded.

Dispatch information may include:
- quantity
- date
- destination
- transport information

WEIGHING
Coal quantities should be measured using suitable weighing arrangements.

Weighing records should be maintained where required.

STOCK
Coal stocks should be monitored periodically.

Differences between recorded and physical stock may require investigation.

REPORTING
Mine operators may be required to submit operational information to relevant authorities.

Reports should be accurate and submitted through the applicable procedure.

SUPERVISION
Mining operations should have appropriate supervision.

Supervisors should monitor operational activities and ensure that applicable procedures are followed.

RECORD RETENTION
Important operational records should be retained for the period required by the applicable rules.
